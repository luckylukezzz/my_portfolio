Featured talk · Sydney, 27 September – 1 October 2026

Presenting at Interspeech 2026 CORE A

Interspeech is the flagship conference of the International Speech Communication Association. I presented our work orally in Sydney,

Causal Redundancy in Speech Representations: The Hydra Effect and the Limits of Sparse Disentanglement in WavLM.

The project began as our final-year undergraduate thesis. We study how WavLM encodes information such as pitch and tone, and why that information is so difficult to separate cleanly with current sparse-disentanglement methods.

Co-authors: Kasindu Bandara, Vilash Nawagamuwa, Janak Senevirathne, Uthayasanker Thayasivam.

Research projects

2025 – 2026 · Final-year research project

Explainable Voice Analysis Framework for Speech Models

A model-agnostic explainability framework for WavLM and Wav2Vec with layer-wise and neuron-wise analysis. Combines probing with MDL control, SHAP with sparse autoencoders for neuron-level causal analysis, and INLP subspace erasure for targeted feature removal. Includes an interactive visualisation tool (Flask, JavaScript).

2025 · Research project

Few-Shot Adaptation of Multimodal Foundation Models

An empirical study of adapting Contrastive Captioners (CoCa) to low-resource visual recognition, comparing LoRA, hybrid prototype classification and linear probing. The best results came from combining supervised contrastive loss with cross-entropy.
